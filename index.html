VALUATION METHOD

Value = Book value × (ROE − g) / (Ke − g)

ROE is sustainable return on equity, Ke is your required equity return, and g is long-run growth. The result is sensitive to each input, especially the gap between Ke and g.

06 / INVESTMENT RESEARCH

What could change the result?

01

Why the market pays a premium

DBS has historically generated strong returns on equity, supported by its deposit base and wealth-management business. The estimate assumes a material portion of that profitability persists.

02

What would invalidate the base case

Structurally lower ROE, sustained net-interest margin compression, rising credit costs, or a higher required equity return would reduce estimated value.

03

Capital return is not recurring income

The additional quarterly dividend distributes accumulated excess capital. It should not be treated as a permanent component of ordinary dividend growth.
